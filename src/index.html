How logging works.

  1. 01
    Choose a procedure

    Search the orthopedic catalog or tap an anatomic region.

  2. 02
    Add the fields that apply

    Record your involvement, approach, technique, graft, components and any other details specific to that procedure.

  3. 03
    Add images and notes

    Attach pre-op, intra-op and post-op images, each with a note about what happened.

  4. 04
    Save the case

    The case stays searchable by date, rotation, site, surgeon and subspecialty.

  5. 05
    Export when you need it

    Create a spreadsheet for review or an offline case book with images.
